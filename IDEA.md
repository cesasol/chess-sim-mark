# I started as a pawn

A dating sim 3d game with chess as the core mechanic for interaction and progress.

Dual gender focus bishōjo and otome style

## MVP Scope

- Unreal Engine
- Stunning graphics
- Free assets for MVP
- Anime inspired narrative following cliches such as yandere and tsundere
- Dual gender preference, male and female character roaster
- Stockfish UCI subprocess integration as a chess engine
- 3D gameplay
- Live character reaction
- csv data loading for character lines, scenarios and scripted flows
- Localization ready
- Mouse and keyboard gameplay
- Linear transition between conversation and chess board
- Art style: Modern JRPG

### First playable MVP

- One complete loop with one opponent: introduction and conversation → full chess
  game with live character reactions → relationship outcome
- Standard chess against adjustable AI; winning advances the story, while losing
  allows a retry
- University setting with all characters explicitly 18+
- Linux-first development and validation; other platforms deferred
- Placeholder assets and text-only dialogue initially, targeting a polished Modern
  JRPG presentation
- Check Stockfish and Unreal Engine licensing compatibility before committing to
  the chess integration architecture

### Initial scenario

A university drama to win the heart of the best player by defeating them. All
characters are explicitly 18+.
